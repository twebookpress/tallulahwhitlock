DRIFTWOOD & CO. VACATIONS • BOOK ONE

Beach House
Rules

Two weeks. One beach house. Absolutely no sharing.

COMING SOON

Some vacations come with a house full of rules. This one comes with a man who refuses to follow them.

Yvette Breck has spent a decade telling people exactly what's wrong with their food and doing it so precisely that chefs cry and editors call it "the most entertaining cruelty in print." She does not share. She does not compromise. And she absolutely does not share a beach house with a man whose family has been encroaching on her family's vacation week for fifteen years.

Jonah Carver teaches ninth-grade history in a way that makes teenagers actually care about dead people, coaches JV soccer because the role was open and leaving things unfilled bothers him more than the inconvenience, and arrived at the beach house this particular summer needing two weeks of nothing. He did not expect the nothing to come with Yvette Breck, her cabinet labels, and her frankly alarming relationship with quiet.

Neither will leave. The property management company is unreachable. And Jonah has discovered—completely by accident—that Yvette's controlled-devastation routine cracks, just slightly, when he makes her laugh.
