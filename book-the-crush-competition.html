One beach house. One old grudge. And one crush that never got its chance.

Ten years ago, Frankie Doyle waited all night on a dock for a boy who never showed.

This August, Driftwood & Co. Vacation Rentals hands them the exact same confirmation number for the exact same house—and neither of them will be the one to leave first.

When they find the shoebox of childhood dares still hidden under the porch swing, “loser leaves” becomes a real rule again.

The trouble is, the deeper they get into that box, the closer they get to the one dare they never opened—and the version of that night Frankie has spent a decade being furious about isn't the one that actually happened.
