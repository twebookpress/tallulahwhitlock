LOVE & LATTES • BOOK TWO

Unicorn Frappuccino
and a Grumpy Barista

A fake fiancé. A grumpy barista. And one very pink problem.

COMING SOON • DRAFTING & EDITING

She orders the unicorn. He makes it every morning. Then one little lie changes everything.

Shane Blue runs the most orderly coffee shop in Cypress Bay, Louisiana—every shot pulled to thirty-one seconds, every book alphabetized to the spine, no chaos and no exceptions.

Then Penny Ford walked in and ordered a Unicorn Frappuccino. Pink, blue, three syrups, edible glitter, eleven minutes. She orders it every morning. He makes it every time. And somewhere along the way it became the structuring event of his day—a fact he has chosen not to examine.

It stops being ignorable on a Friday morning when Penny's ex-boyfriend walks in: Julian Cormier, food critic, festival judge, and the man who spent two years convincing her she was too much.

Penny vaults the counter. And when Julian finds her anyway, armed with that patient, condescending smile, she tells him Shane is her fiancé. The shop goes silent—and then, with the quiet steadiness of a man who doesn't negotiate with people who diminish good people, Shane puts his hand on her back and tells Julian Cormier she is exactly enough.

Now they have one week, four rules, a fake engagement, and an entire small town that already knows. The rules are clear. The deadline is firm. Neither of them is following Rule Three.
