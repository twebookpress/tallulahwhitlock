LOVE & LATTES • BOOK THREE

The Peppermint Mocha
Predicament

Two rival baristas. One Christmas romance brewing.

COMING SOON • DRAFTING & EDITING

A Christmas coffee rivalry, a town-wide festival, and a recipe neither of them could have made alone.

Fiona Beaumont knows two things about December in Mistletoe Bay: it runs on community spirit and Peppermint Mocha, in that order. She's been running her grandmother's beloved coffee shop for eight years, and she has exactly zero interest in the sleek city transplant who just opened a competitor next door and clearly thinks he knows better.

Callan arrived with a spreadsheet, a six-week timeline, and what he'll privately admit was an entirely manageable amount of arrogance. He did not arrive expecting to care about a small coastal town, its mayor's annual Peppermint Mocha habit, or the sharp, warm woman with the cream knit jumper who seems to know every single person in it.

When the town's Christmas festival recruits them both as co-chairs — because Mistletoe Bay has always known exactly what it's doing — Fiona and Callan find themselves sharing committee tables, vendor crises, and the kind of proximity that makes it very difficult to keep pretending the rivalry is the whole story.

There's a coffee competition with their names on it. A storm coming in off the bay. And a recipe neither of them could have made alone.
