Love, Lies and Latte Foam

Love & Lattes • Book One

AVAILABLE NOW

Rival baristas. Grounds for attraction.

Two coffee shops. One competition. And enough chemistry to make things seriously complicated.

Running The Daily Grind is more than a job—it’s Cerise's entire world. Between over-caffeinated regulars, experimental latte flavors, and a community that feels like family, her cozy café is the soul of their small town. So when a sleek corporate coffee shop opens directly across the street, she knows exactly who to blame. Kayce is quiet, impeccably dressed, and annoyingly perfect. He measures espresso shots with scientific precision, never smiles without a reason, and manages the new franchise Blackout Coffee like it’s a military operation. Their rivalry goes public fast—snarky banter, viral latte art battles, and a town-sponsored Golden Bean competition that turns their feud into full-blown entertainment. As forced proximity, public flirting, and one dangerously close near-kiss blur the line between enemies and something much sweeter, Cerise starts to see the man beneath the grumpy exterior. What she doesn’t know is that Kayce is hiding a truth big enough to shatter her trust—and threaten the coffee shop she’s worked her whole life to build. Because he’s not just her rival. He’s the CEO’s son and he was never supposed to fall in love.
